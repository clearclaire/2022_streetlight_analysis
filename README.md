## 🗂️ Project Overview
This project analyzes streetlight distribution across districts in Busan, South Korea, in response to rising crime rates and safety concerns.  
Using CPTED (Crime Prevention Through Environmental Design) as a framework, we propose lighting improvements to foster safer urban environments.

## 📊 Key Findings

- Identified high-risk districts in Busan based on:
  - Proportion of foreign residents and female population
  - Crime occurrence rates and CCTV coverage
  - Lighting infrastructure in residential zones

- Used CPTED (Crime Prevention Through Environmental Design) framework to interpret findings:
  - Weak access control and surveillance in key areas
  - Insufficient community engagement and public infrastructure

- Suggested areas for improvement:
  - Increase street lighting and visibility
  - Install additional CCTV in blind spots
  - Encourage resident involvement in neighborhood maintenance

*Note: Project focused on data interpretation and visualization rather than direct implementation.*
